
# Excess Baggage Calculator

A Flutter mobile application developed for estimating excess baggage charges for air travel.

## Application Features

- Enter checked baggage weight in kilograms.
- Select Economy or Business ticket class.
- Enter excess baggage rate per kilogram (RM).
- Calculate excess baggage weight.
- Calculate excess baggage charges.
- Calculate 8% service tax.
- Display total amount payable.
- Input validation for empty, negative and invalid values.
- Home Page and About Page navigation.

## Baggage Allowance

| Ticket Class | Baggage Allowance |
|---|---:|
| Economy | 20 kg |
| Business | 40 kg |

## Calculation Formula

Excess Weight = Actual Weight - Baggage Allowance

Excess Charge = Excess Weight × Rate per KG

Tax = Excess Charge × 8%

Total Payable = Excess Charge + Tax

If baggage weight does not exceed the allowance, the excess charge is RM0.00.
